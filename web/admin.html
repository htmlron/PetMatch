Basic Information

Use the pet's approximate kilo and height to decide the size.
  • 0 - None: Naturally low-maintenance or no grooming routine needed.
  • 1 - Minimal: Very light coat care; brushing or cleaning is rarely needed.
  • 2 - Low: Occasional brushing and basic hygiene are enough.
  • 3 - Moderate: Regular brushing and routine care keep the pet comfortable.
  • 4 - High: Frequent brushing, baths, or coat maintenance are needed.
  • 5 - Very High: Intensive grooming, trimming, or frequent maintenance is required.

Use coat type, shedding, tangles, cleanliness, and maintenance needs as your guide.

  • 0 - Low shedding: Minimal shedding; easy to keep floors and clothes clean.
  • 1 - Light shedding: Occasional fur; quick brushing helps.
  • 2 - Some fur: Moderate shedding; lint rollers and regular brushing recommended.
  • 3 - Noticeable shedding: Fur is common around the home; frequent brushing helps a lot.
  • 4 - Lots of fur: Heavy shedding or seasonal blowouts; expect regular cleanup.
  • 5 - Heavy shedding: Very heavy shedding; fur on furniture/clothes is expected.

Use coat length/density, seasonal shedding, and how quickly fur builds up in the home as your guide.

Health & Behavior

Personality

  • 0 - Very Calm: Sleeps or rests most of the day and rarely seeks activity.
  • 1 - Low: Relaxed and steady; only short bursts of movement.
  • 2 - Light: Enjoys short walks and mild play without much intensity.
  • 3 - Moderate: Balanced energy; likes a normal amount of daily activity.
  • 4 - High: Active and lively; needs regular exercise and engagement.
  • 5 - Very High: Extremely energetic and needs strong daily outlets.

Judge by movement, stamina, rest patterns, and how much activity the pet seeks.

  • 0 - None: Shows little or no interest in toys or play.
  • 1 - Very Low: Rarely initiates play, but may join briefly.
  • 2 - Low: Enjoys occasional short play sessions.
  • 3 - Moderate: Responds well to toys, games, and interaction.
  • 4 - High: Actively seeks play and engagement.
  • 5 - Very High: Loves frequent interactive play and stimulation.

Look at toy interest, chase behavior, pouncing, and how often the pet asks to play.

  • 0 - Minimal: Avoids contact and prefers to be left alone.
  • 1 - Low: Accepts little contact and is cautious with affection.
  • 2 - Mild: Tolerates gentle attention and light petting.
  • 3 - Moderate: Enjoys interaction and responds well to affection.
  • 4 - High: Frequently seeks cuddles and physical closeness.
  • 5 - Very High: Loves constant contact and being held or petted.

Use meet-and-greet behavior, approach response, body relaxation, and contact seeking.

  • 0 - Dependent: Needs constant company and reassurance.
  • 1 - Very Low: Strongly attached and dislikes being alone.
  • 2 - Low: Prefers presence and regular check-ins.
  • 3 - Moderate: Handles some alone time comfortably.
  • 4 - High: Comfortable with independence and routine alone time.
  • 5 - Very High: Highly independent and relaxed when alone.

Think about separation comfort, attachment level, and how often the pet seeks human presence.

  • 0 - Very Low: Struggles with change and new environments.
  • 1 - Low: Needs lots of time and support to adjust.
  • 2 - Mild: Can adapt slowly when introduced carefully.
  • 3 - Moderate: Adjusts to new routines with some help.
  • 4 - High: Adapts well to new people, places, and routines.
  • 5 - Very High: Extremely flexible and settles in quickly.

Consider stress response, routine changes, travel, and how quickly the pet settles in.

  • 0 - Very Easy: Learns quickly and responds well to training.
  • 1 - Easy: Picks up commands with light guidance.
  • 2 - Mild: Needs some repetition but learns steadily.
  • 3 - Moderate: Requires consistent training and patience.
  • 4 - Challenging: Takes longer to learn and may resist new commands.
  • 5 - Very Challenging: Needs strong consistency and experienced handling.

Use response to cues, consistency, obedience, and how much repetition the pet needs.
